Jessica Dunlap, and her hometown

My hometown is an interesting place, with an even more peculiar history. I havent lived in La Grange in over a year, and i miss it dearly.

History of La Grange, TX

La Grange might be best known by the world for the ZZ Top song or the Chicken Ranch. That is if anyone knows of it at all. however, I know it as farm land stretching as far as you can see, friendly neighbors, and a lovely community.

Why so nostalgic now?

Honestly, on a day to day basis i dont really long for the small town lifestyle. With that being said, the last couple weeks in austin have been hectic, and i find myself overwhelmed frequently. This weekend La Grange is hosting the Fayette County Fair, and i am not sure if i will be able to make it. If i do not make it, it will be the first year i have ever missed the fair.
